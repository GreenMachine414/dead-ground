Surviving

  • Zombies come in rounds. Each round has more, tougher zombies. Clear the round to get a short break.
  • Earn points for every hit and kill. Spend them on the Mystery Box (950), perk machines, clearing debris, and the Weapon Forge, which upgrades your current gun to X (5000), then to XX (10000, after 100 kills with the X gun).
  • Guns are found by searching weapon racks, lockers, crates, car trunks and fallen survivors (look for the ✦ sparkle, hold E). Guns roll a rarity — Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary — with more damage, bigger magazines and faster reloads. Military chests always hold Rare or better. Searched containers sometimes restock between rounds.
  • Kills earn XP. Each level, pick one of three upgrades (1/2/3) — they stack for the rest of the run.
  • Zombies drop power-ups: Max Ammo, Insta-Kill, Double Points and Kaboom.
  • Follow the objective (top-left, yellow beacons and the minimap) to progress the story and escape. Every map has a boss. Beat Ground Zero's story to unlock Watch the Finale under the map list.
  • Runs earn Salvage; spend it in Characters & Upgrades to level up each survivor's ability.
  • In co-op, every guest readies up in the lobby before the host can start. A downed teammate has 45 seconds: follow the red revive icon (on screen, at the screen edge with distance and time left, and on the minimap) and hold E next to them. Dead players respawn next round.
  • In co-op the pause menu doesn't stop the game for everyone else.
  • Errors and crashes are reported automatically, or send a report yourself with Bug Reports/Feedback.

Graphics

  • High (default): full detail. It lowers its resolution by itself if the game starts to slow down.
  • Low: fewer pixels and particles for the best frame rate on slower PCs.
  • Changes apply instantly, from the main menu or the pause menu during a game.
